# GCC Deal Qualification

BANT framework adapted for Gulf business cycles and decision-making patterns.

## Standard BANT + GCC Qualifiers

### Budget

| GCC Context | What to Check | How to Check |
|-------------|--------------|--------------|
| Government fiscal year | Varies by country (UAE: Jan-Dec, Saudi: same) | Ask procurement contact |
| Budget cycle | Budgets set 6-12 months in advance | "When does your fiscal year start?" |
| Procurement process | Formal RFP/RFI process required | "What's your procurement process for this type of solution?" |
| Sovereign wealth | Long timelines, but deep pockets | "Who approves budgets of this size?" |
| Family offices | Faster decisions, less process | "Is this something you can greenlight directly?" |

**Scoring:**
- Budget confirmed + allocated = **Hot**
- Budget exists but not allocated = **Warm**
- No budget cycle identified = **Nurture**
- Budget cycle is 6+ months away = **Nurture**

### Authority

| GCC Structure | Decision Chain | What It Means |
|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| Family-owned business | Owner → CEO → department head | Owner can override anyone |
| Government entity | Director → VP → minister → cabinet | Multi-layer approval, slow |
| Free zone company | CEO → CTO/VP → manager | Flatter, faster (Western-style) |
| Sovereign wealth | Investment committee → board | Formal, process-driven |
| MNC regional office | Regional HQ → global HQ | Decisions may leave the country |

**Scoring:**
- Met final decision-maker = **Hot**
- Met budget holder, need technical buy-in = **Warm**
- Met influencer only = **Nurture**
- Can't identify decision chain = **Block**

### Need

| GCC Pain Points | Signals | Your Solution Angle |
|-----------------|---------|---------------------|
| Digital transformation mandates | Vision 2030, National Strategy | "We help you meet your national strategy goals" |
| Talent shortage | Hiring posts, high turnover | "We supplement your team while you build capability" |
| Vendor consolidation | Multiple tools, integration pain | "One platform to replace three" |
| Compliance requirements | PDPL, SAMA, data localization | "We're compliant out of the box" |
| Cost optimization | Budget pressure, efficiency mandates | "Reduce costs by X% while improving Y" |

**Scoring:**
- Pain is urgent + validated by multiple sources = **Hot**
- Pain exists but not yet prioritized = **Warm**
- Pain is hypothetical or unvalidated = **Nurture**

### Timeline

| GCC Timeline Reality | What to Expect |
|---------------------|----------------|
| Ramadan | Decisions freeze. Don't expect progress. |
| Summer exodus (July-Aug) | Key stakeholders may be unavailable |
| National days | Government entities close for 3-5 days |
| End of fiscal year | Budget flush (spend remaining) or freeze (new budget) |
| Hajj season | Saudi Arabia slows down significantly |

**Scoring:**
- Decision within 30 days = **Hot**
- Decision within 90 days = **Warm**
- Decision within 6 months = **Nurture**
- No clear timeline = **Block**

## Disqualification Criteria

Disqualify (or downweight) if:

- Government entity with no active tender/budget cycle
- Family office with no relevant use case
- Company in financial distress (recent layoffs, contract losses)
- Decision-maker has no budget authority and no path to one
- Timeline exceeds 12 months with no catalyst
- Cultural mismatch (e.g., you can't provide Arabic support and they need it)

## When to Walk Away

- After 3 failed attempts to reach the decision-maker
- If they explicitly say "not interested" (respect it — don't ghost)
- If the deal requires something you genuinely can't deliver
- If the cultural fit is wrong (e.g., they expect a local partner and you don't have one)
