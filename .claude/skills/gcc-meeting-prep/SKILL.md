# GCC Meeting Prep

Pre-meeting research template calibrated for GCC business culture.

## Pre-Meeting Research Checklist

### Company Research (15 minutes)

- [ ] **Basic info:** Legal name, HQ location, size, revenue (if public)
- [ ] **Ownership:** Family-owned? Government-linked? Public? Sovereign wealth stake?
- [ ] **Recent news:** Last 3 months — funding, contracts, leadership changes, expansion
- [ ] **Tech stack:** What tools/platforms they use (check job postings, LinkedIn, BuiltWith)
- [ ] **Competitors:** Who are they competing with? What's their market position?
- [ ] **Procurement process:** Government entity? Free zone? Private? (affects decision speed)

### Decision-Maker Dossier (10 minutes)

- [ ] **Name:** Full name, correct transliteration, pronunciation guide
- [ ] **Title:** Exact title — don't guess hierarchy
- [ ] **LinkedIn:** Last 5 posts/activity — what do they care about?
- [ ] **Background:** Previous companies, education, mutual connections
- [ ] **Interests:** Hobbies, causes, published articles (if available)
- [ ] **Communication style:** Formal or informal? Data-driven or relationship-driven?

### Cultural Protocol (5 minutes)

- [ ] **Country-specific norms:** UAE (Western-leaning), Saudi (more formal), Qatar (intermediate)
- [ ] **Meeting format:** In-person, video call, or phone? (In-person is preferred for first meetings)
- [ ] **Gift policy:** Some entities prohibit gifts. Ask your champion.
- [ ] **Dress code:** Business formal for government, business casual for tech companies
- [ ] **Language:** English OK? Or need Arabic? (Most UAE/Qatar tech = English; Saudi gov = Arabic)

## Meeting Agenda Template

```
[Company] — Meeting with [Decision-Maker Name]
Date: [Date] | Time: [Time GST/AST] | Duration: [30/45/60 min]
Location/Platform: [Office address / Zoom link]

AGENDA
──────
1. Opening & Relationship Building (5 min)
   - Personal connection point (from dossier)
   - Their current priorities/challenges

2. Their Situation (10 min)
   - What's working well for them?
   - What challenges are they facing?
   - What are their goals for next 6-12 months?

3. Our Capabilities (10 min)
   - Relevant case study (GCC-specific if possible)
   - Key differentiators for their context
   - Demo or walkthrough (if applicable)

4. Next Steps (5 min)
   - What would they need to see to move forward?
   - Who else should be involved?
   - Timeline and process

5. Closing & Follow-Up (5 min)
   - Summary of discussion
   - Agreed next steps
   - Thank them for their time
```

## Leave-Behind Materials

Prepare these in advance:

| Material | Format | When to Use |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Executive summary | 1-page PDF | Leave after meeting; email follow-up |
| Case study (GCC) | 2-3 page PDF | If they ask for proof points |
| ROI calculator | Excel/interactive | If budget is a concern |
| Technical overview | PDF or demo | If technical team is involved |
| Company brochure | PDF | First meeting only |

**Rules:**
- All materials should be in English AND Arabic (if government entity)
- Use metric units (not imperial)
- Include GCC-specific examples, not US/Europe examples
- Print high-quality copies for in-person meetings

## Post-Meeting Actions

Within 24 hours:

- [ ] Send thank-you email referencing specific discussion points
- [ ] Share any promised materials
- [ ] Update CRM with notes and next steps
- [ ] Connect on LinkedIn (if not already connected)
- [ ] Set calendar reminder for follow-up

Within 1 week:

- [ ] Deliver on any promises made in the meeting
- [ ] Send relevant content/insight (not a pitch)
- [ ] Check if they have questions or need additional info
