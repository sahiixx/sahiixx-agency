# GCC Referral Network

Building and leveraging referral relationships in the Gulf.

## Wasta — The Connection Economy

**Wasta** (واسطة) means "connection" or "influence" — it's the informal network of relationships that make business happen in the GCC.

**Ethical wasta:**
- Introductions to the right people
- Recommending based on genuine merit
- Opening doors that would otherwise stay closed

**Unethical wasta:**
- Bribery or favoritism
- Bypassing legitimate processes
- Using connections to avoid compliance

**Rule:** Use wasta to get a meeting. Never use it to close a deal unfairly.

## Networking Event Playbook

**Before the event:**
- Research attendees and speakers (LinkedIn, event app)
- Identify 3-5 people you want to meet
- Prepare your 30-second intro (in English AND Arabic if possible)
- Bring business cards (still important in GCC)

**During the event:**
- Arrive on time (being late is disrespectful)
- Listen more than you talk
- Ask about THEIR work, not yours
- Exchange business cards with both hands (or right hand)
- Don't check your phone during conversations

**After the event:**
- Send LinkedIn connection requests within 24 hours
- Reference something specific from your conversation
- Suggest a follow-up coffee or call
- Add them to your CRM with notes
