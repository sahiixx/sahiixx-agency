# Sponsor Reply Email Process

Step 1: Read the incoming email I paste. Identify what they want:
  - Pricing inquiry
  - Past work request
  - Booking a specific slot
  - Negotiating a deal

Step 2: Pull my current rate card from what-i-do.md. Pull recent wins from operating-context.md.

Step 3: Draft a reply that:
  - Answers their direct question in the first sentence
  - Anchors with one specific recent win
  - Names the next clear step
  - Stays under 80 words

Step 4: Flag any email that needs my personal voice. Don't draft those. Just summarize and ask me to handle it.
