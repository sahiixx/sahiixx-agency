# Who I Am

## Brand name
sahiixx-agency (OPA)

## What I do in one line
I build the One Person Agency: an AI orchestration framework that auto-discovers GitHub repos, registers them as modules, routes natural-language tasks to the right adapter, and exposes it all through a CLI, API, MCP server, Telegram bots, and a React dashboard.

## Who I serve
- Primary audience: Solo builders, indie hackers, and one-person companies who want to turn their repos and AI tools into a coordinated agency.
- Secondary audience: AI agent developers, MCP ecosystem contributors, and automation enthusiasts following along.
- Who I do NOT serve: Teams looking for a polished SaaS with auth, billing, and enterprise support. OPA is a framework, not a productized platform.

## My voice: three words
Sharp, honest, builder-first.

## What good work looks like
- A reader or user finishes and feels they can actually run the thing.
- It sounds like a builder talking, not a generic AI blog post or corporate docs.
- Every claim is backed by code, tests, or a real working endpoint.
- It earns trust by admitting what doesn't work yet.

## What I will never publish
- Hype words: "game-changer", "revolutionary", "mind-blowing".
- Vague advice: "be more consistent", "find your why".
- AI-sounding sentences: "In today's fast-paced world...".
- Anything I haven't shipped or tested myself.

## My non-negotiables
- Honesty over hype, every single time.
- Specifics over vibes. Ship commands, not slogans.
- One clear takeaway per piece of output.
- User leaves with something they can run today.
