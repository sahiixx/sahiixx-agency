# GCC Market Intelligence

How to gather competitive and market intelligence in the Gulf region.

## Government Tender Portals

| Country | Portal | URL | What's There |
|---------|--------|-----|-------------|
| UAE | Munafasat | munafasat.gov.ae | Federal government tenders |
| UAE | Dubai Procurement | dubai.gov.ae | Dubai government tenders |
| Saudi Arabia | Etimad | etimad.sa | All government procurement |
| Saudi Arabia | Etimad (EN) | etimad.sa/en | English version |
| Qatar | Qatar Government tenders | gov.qa | Government contracts |
| Kuwait | Kuwait Tenderboard | tenderboard.gov.kw | Government tenders |
| Bahrain | Bahrain tender portal | tender.gov.bh | Government tenders |
| Oman | Oman Tender Board | tenderboard.gov.om | Government tenders |

**How to use:**
- Check weekly for new tenders in your category
- Set up email alerts where available
- Note bid deadlines — GCC government deadlines are often strict
- Understand evaluation criteria (price vs. quality weighting)

## Social Listening (GCC-Specific)

**Twitter/X is dominant in GCC** — more than LinkedIn for news and opinions.

| Platform | GCC Usage | What to Monitor |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Twitter/X | Very high (especially UAE, Saudi) | Industry conversations, company announcements, executive opinions |
| LinkedIn | High (B2B) | Job postings, company updates, executive moves |
| Instagram | High (consumer) | Brand presence, company culture |
| Snapchat | High (Saudi youth) | Less relevant for B2B |
| TikTok | Growing | Brand awareness, less B2B |

**Keywords to monitor:**
- Company names (competitors + prospects)
- Industry terms in English AND Arabic
- Government officials in your sector
- Vision 2030 + your industry
- Competitor brand names

For each competitor in the GCC:

```
- HQ location: [Country]

- [Strength 1]
- [Strength 2]

WEAKNESSES:
- [Weakness 1]

- [How we're different in GCC context]
```

## Data Sources Quick Reference

- **Company financials:** Zawya, D&B, annual reports (if public)
- **Job postings:** LinkedIn, company careers pages
- **Tech stack:** BuiltWith, Wappalyzer, job posting requirements
- **News:** Arab News, Gulf Business, Zawya, Twitter/X
- **Tenders:** Government portals listed above
- **Social:** Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram
