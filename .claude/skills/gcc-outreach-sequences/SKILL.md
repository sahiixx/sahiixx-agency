# GCC Outreach Sequences

Multi-touch sequence templates calibrated for GCC business culture.

## Cultural Foundation

**GCC buyers are relationship-first.** The sequence must build trust before asking for anything.

- **Formality:** Start formal (Mr./Ms./Dr.), relax only after they signal comfort
- **Language:** English is standard for B2B in UAE/Qatar. Arabic for Saudi government entities.
- **Timing:** Avoid Friday (holy day) and Saturday (weekend in UAE/Qatar). Sunday-Thursday is the work week.
- **Wasta matters:** A warm intro beats any cold outreach. Always check for mutual connections first.

## Sequence 1: LinkedIn-First (recommended for most prospects)

### Touch 1 — Connection Request (Day 1)
```
Hi [First Name],

I noticed [specific trigger — their post, company news, role change].
[1-2 sentence relevant observation or compliment].

Would love to connect and follow your work at [Company].

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**Rules:**
- Max 300 characters (connection request limit)
- NO pitch in the connection request
- Reference something specific — generic requests get ignored
- Include your real name and company

### Touch 2 — Thank You Message (Day 2-3, after they accept)
```
Thanks for connecting, [First Name].

[Reference something from their profile/post — show you actually looked].

I work with [type of companies] in the GCC on [1-sentence value prop].
Happy to share insights anytime — no pitch, just genuine interest in what you're building.

[Your Name]
```

### Touch 3 — Value-Add Message (Day 7-10)
```
Hi [First Name],

Saw [relevant news/trigger about their company or industry].

We helped [similar company] solve [specific problem] — [1-sentence result].
Thought it might be relevant given [their specific situation].

Would a 15-minute call this week make sense to explore?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Touch 4 — Breakup Message (Day 21-28)
```
Hi [First Name],

I've reached out a few times about [topic] — I understand timing is everything.

If now isn't the right time, no worries at all. I'll keep sharing relevant insights periodically.

If you'd like to revisit this conversation later, I'm here.

Wishing you and the [Company] team continued success.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## Sequence 2: Email-First (for prospects with known email)

### Email 1 — Introduction (Day 1)
```
Subject: [Company] + [Your Company] — quick question

Dear [Mr./Ms. Last Name],

I hope this message finds you well.

I'm [Your Name] from [Your Company]. We work with [type of companies] in the [industry] sector across the GCC.

I noticed [specific trigger — recent news, job posting, LinkedIn activity].
[1-2 sentences connecting their situation to your expertise].

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute call to discuss how we might support [Company]'s [specific initiative]?

I'm available [2-3 time slots in Gulf Standard Time].

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
[Title]
[Company]
[Phone — include country code]
```

### Email 2 — Value Add (Day 5)
```
Subject: Re: [Company] + [Your Company] — quick question

Dear [Mr./Ms. Last Name],

Following up on my previous message. I wanted to share [a relevant resource — case study, report, insight] that might be useful:

[Link or attachment]

We've seen this approach work well for [similar company/type of company] in [country].

Happy to discuss how it might apply to [Company] if you're interested.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### Email 3 — Breakup (Day 14)
```
Subject: Closing the loop

Dear [Mr./Ms. Last Name],

I understand you're busy — I'll keep this brief.

If [topic] isn't a priority right now, no problem. I'll reach out again in [quarter/month] when the timing might be better.

In the meantime, I wish you and the team at [Company] all the best.

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
```

## Sequence 3: WhatsApp (use only when culturally appropriate)

**When to use:** UAE/Qatar private sector, after LinkedIn connection established, or if they shared their WhatsApp.

**When NOT to use:** Government entities, Saudi Arabia (more formal), first touch.

### WhatsApp 1 — Soft Touch
```
Hi [First Name] 👋

[Your Name] here from [Company] — we connected on LinkedIn.

Just wanted to say hello and share this [relevant article/insight]:
[Link]

No rush on回复 — just thought you might find it interesting.

Have a great day!
```

**Rules:**
- One message max — don't spam
- Use emojis sparingly (1-2 per message)
- Never send voice notes without permission
- Respond within 24 hours if they reply

## A/B Testing Notes

- **Subject lines:** Test Arabic names transliterated vs. English names
- **Formality:** Test "Dear Mr." vs. "Hi [First Name]" — track response rates by company size
- **Timing:** Test morning (9am) vs. afternoon (2pm) sends
- **Length:** Test short (3 lines) vs. medium (5-7 lines) — GCC buyers tend to prefer slightly more context than Western buyers
